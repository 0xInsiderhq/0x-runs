Make a 30-second black-and-white cartoon in the style of a late-1920s rubber-hose short, entirely in code, from this one prompt. No stock footage, no downloaded art, no image or video generators. Everything seen and heard must be made by your own code in the sandbox.

Character: invent your own. Not a mouse, not a cat, and it must not resemble any existing cartoon character, studio mascot or logo.

Story: a setup, two gags that escalate, a payoff. Write the beat sheet first, then build it.

Style:
- rubber-hose limbs, bouncy squash and stretch
- animated on twos, 12 new drawings per second, played at 24 fps
- visible line boil between drawings
- film look: grain, gate weave, flicker, dust and scratches, soft vignette
- opens on a title card with a title you invent, ends on an iris-out

Sound, also made in code: the music is your call. Pick whatever genre, tempo and instruments you think fit the film best, but it must be your own original composition, no existing melodies. Add sound effects that hit exactly on the actions.

Deliverable: one MP4, exactly 30.0 s, 1440x1080, 24 fps, H.264 + AAC, about -16 LUFS. Check it with ffprobe before you hand it over.

Work like a one-person animation studio: render small previews, look at your own frames, fix what is wrong, render again. At the end report: total time, how many drawings you actually made, how many frames are exact repeats, what music you chose and why, what you fixed after looking at previews, and what you would still fix.
